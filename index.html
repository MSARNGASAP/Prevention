🛠️ Tactical Coping Skills

Tactical (Box) Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds. Hold for 4 seconds. Exhale for 4 seconds. Hold for 4 seconds. Repeat 4 times to lower your heart rate.

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Find: 5 things you see, 4 things you can touch, 3 things you hear, 2 things you smell, 1 thing you taste. Use this to stop a downward spiral.

Control the Controllables

Focus only on your actions, your attitude, and your responses right now. Let go of the past and other people's decisions.
