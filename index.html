Tactical Coping Tools

WHEN TO USE: Acute Panic or Extreme Anger

1. Tactical (Box) Breathing

Trigger points: Use this when you feel a racing heart, sudden panic, overwhelming rage, hyperventilation, or right before a confrontation. It physically forces your nervous system to calm down.

Follow the expanding circle below: Inhale (4s), Hold (4s), Exhale (4s), Hold (4s).

BREATHE
Follow the pace to regulate heart rate
WHEN TO USE: Mental Spirals or Flashbacks

2. 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Method

Trigger points: Use this when trapped in anxiety-driven thought spirals, flashbacks, feeling detached from reality, or experiencing intrusive "what-if" thoughts. It pulls your brain back into the physical room.

Stop and actively identify:
👀 5 things you can see around you.
✋ 4 things you can physically touch.
👂 3 things you can hear right now.
👃 2 things you can smell.
👅 1 thing you can taste.

WHEN TO USE: Command/Admin Frustration

3. Control the Controllables

Trigger points: Use this when overwhelmed by delayed paperwork, sudden unit changes, frustrating leadership decisions, or things out of your immediate hand.

Draw a line down a piece of paper. Focus ONLY on column A:

🟢 A: What is in MY control: My effort, my daily physical routine, my reactions to others, how I treat my peers, taking care of my kit.

🔴 B: What is OUT of my control: Command timelines, Guard Bureau decisions, administrative delays, other people's behaviors, the past.

WHEN TO USE: "Worst-Case" Catastrophizing

4. Break Catastrophic Thinking

Trigger points: Use this when you are convinced a bad situation (like failing an evaluation or a relationship fight) means your entire career or life is instantly over.

Answer these 3 logic questions directly to reset your brain:
1. What is the worst-case scenario? (Let yourself face it)
2. What is the absolute best-case scenario? (Usually unrealistic)
3. What is the MOST LIKELY scenario? (The realistic middle ground you can prepare for)
